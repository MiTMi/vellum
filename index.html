¶ Features

Everything a workspace should be —
nothing it shouldn't.

A block editor

Type / for headings, lists, code, math, callouts and embeds. Every block drags, nests and copies as a deep link.

Databases & views

Table, board, calendar, gallery and timeline over the same rows. Relations, rollups and formulas compute as you look at them.

Offline-first

Reads and writes hit a local replica first, so the app never waits on the network. Queued edits replay when you reconnect.

Publish to web

One toggle turns a page into a public link. Unpublishing revokes it permanently — the link is the access control.

⌘K everything

Full-text search with contextual snippets, plus the actions you reach for most — new page, new database, theme, trash.

Native Mac app

The same workspace as a real desktop app: native menus, Touch ID unlock, proper PDF export and its own Dock icon.

Built-in AI

Fix a sentence, ask your notes a question, or have the agent draft whole pages and databases — applied only when you click Apply. Web search is opt-in, safety-checked and logged.

Share with people

Invite someone to a page — and everything inside it — as a viewer or an editor. Take access back anytime and it vanishes from their app on its own.

History, comments & export

Page history you can restore from, comments in the margin, and one-click export to Markdown, HTML, CSV or PDF. Your thoughts are not a subscription.

¶ Vault

For your eyes only.

The Vault is the corner of your workspace that is end-to-end encrypted. Pages inside are sealed on your device with a passphrase only you know — the server, the database and the wire only ever see ciphertext.

No back door, no recovery email. Forget the passphrase and the Vault stays shut — that's the promise. It locks itself after fifteen minutes of quiet, and always on reload.

¶ Write

Write with blocks, not boxes.

Everything on a page is a block, and every block is one keystroke away. Press / for headings, checklists, code, LaTeX, callouts, tables of contents or a live embed.

Mention another page with @ and it stays in sync — rename the target and every mention follows.

¶ Organize

Databases that think with you.

A database is just a page whose children are rows — so every row opens as a full document, with its own content and comments.

Point a relation at another database, roll its values up, or write a formula like prop("Points") * 2. Computed columns are evaluated as you look at them, never stored stale.

¶ Assist

An assistant that does things.

Press ⌘J on a selection to fix, translate or rewrite it. Open the chat panel and ask questions — answers come from your own notes, with citations.

Ask for something bigger and the agent proposes a plan — a database here, a page of content there — and nothing happens until you click Apply. Flip the globe and it can search the web too: opt-in, checked by an independent safety filter, and always logged.

¶ Sync

Works offline. Syncs everywhere.

Vellum keeps a full replica of your workspace on every device. Reads come from the replica, writes land there instantly and queue in a durable outbox.

Reconnect and the queue replays in order — so the Mac app and the browser converge on the same workspace without you thinking about it once.

¶ Together

Your people, your pages.

Vellum is invite-only: mint a code and someone you trust gets their own private workspace — separate accounts, separate pages, nothing shared by accident.

When you want to work together, share a page and everything inside it as a viewer or an editor. Take access back anytime and it disappears from their app on its own. The Vault can never be shared — by design.

¶ Publish

Public when you're ready.

Flip one switch and a page gets an unguessable public link that anyone can read — no account, no app, just the page.

Links to your other pages render as plain titles, so a published page can never leak the private ones. Unpublish and the URL is dead for good.

¶ Three promises

Private by default.

Accounts are invite-only, sharing is a deliberate act, page by page — and the Vault goes further: end-to-end encrypted, readable by no one but you.

Offline by design.

The whole workspace lives on your device. The network is an optimization, not a requirement.

Yours to keep.

Export any page as Markdown, HTML or PDF whenever you like. Your thoughts are not a subscription.
