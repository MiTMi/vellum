Privacy Policy

Last updated August 12, 2026. The short version: your notes are yours, they live on a European server, the Vault is encrypted so that no one — including the operator — can read it, and nothing is sold or used for advertising. Ever.

¶What Vellum stores

  • Your account: an email address and a salted, hashed password. Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • Your content: pages, databases, uploaded files, page history and comments, stored in a Convex database hosted in the EU (eu-west).
  • Sharing records: who you have shared a page with, and at what level.
  • A copy on your devices: Vellum is offline-first, so each device you sign in on keeps a local replica of your workspace.

¶The Vault is different

Pages inside the Vault are end-to-end encrypted on your device with a passphrase that never leaves it. The server stores only ciphertext. Nobody — not the operator, not the hosting provider, not the AI — can read Vault content. There is no recovery if the passphrase is lost; that is the design, not an oversight.

¶Honesty about the operator

Vellum is run by one person for an invited circle. Outside the Vault, content on the server is technically readable by the operator, the same way any hosted service's data is readable by whoever runs it. It isn't read, but you should know the boundary — and put anything that must be private beyond doubt in the Vault.

¶When you use the AI

  • Text you explicitly send to the AI (a selection, a chat message, a page used as context) is processed by a language model via OpenRouter under its API terms. Vellum sends only what you invoked the AI on, keeps no copy beyond your workspace, and Vault pages can never be sent.
  • If you turn the globe on, web searches composed by the AI are sent to Tavily, a search provider. Per Tavily's policy, query text is retained by them while the API account exists. Only short search phrases are sent — never your pages.
  • Outgoing web searches and fetches are recorded in an audit log (who, what, allowed or declined) visible only to the workspace owner, and deleted after 90 days.

¶What Vellum doesn't do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics scripts, no selling or sharing data with anyone beyond the processors named above (Convex for the database, OpenRouter for AI, Tavily for opt-in search).
  • No cookies beyond what sign-in requires; preferences (theme, panel sizes, AI persona) live in your browser's local storage, on your device.

¶Your controls

  • Export any page as Markdown, HTML, CSV or PDF at any time.
  • Delete your account in Settings: it erases your pages, files, history, comments, shares and audit entries in one transaction. This is immediate and irreversible.
  • Published pages are public only while published; unpublishing permanently kills the link.

Last updated August 12, 2026. Your invitation grants a personal, non-transferable license to use Vellum. Here is what that covers — and what it never covers.

¶Allowed

  • Using your workspace for personal notes, documents, databases and files, on any of your devices.
  • Sharing pages with other account holders, and publishing your own content to the web.
  • Using the AI features, including opt-in web access, for lawful purposes.
  • Exporting your content at any time, in any offered format.

¶Not allowed

  • Storing, sharing or publishing content that is illegal, or that infringes someone else's rights.
  • Using any feature — including AI web search and page fetching — to seek or distribute illegal, sexually explicit or violently graphic material, or to harass, stalk, dox or impersonate anyone.

  • Passing your account or invite codes to others without the operator's agreement.

¶Enforcement

Outgoing AI web activity is safety-checked and logged (see the Privacy Policy). Misuse can mean losing web access, AI access, or the account, depending on severity — proportionate, but at the operator's discretion.
